About LineSig

Engineered flow. Protected systems. Assured performance.

LINESIG is an engineering-focused company delivering equipment solutions and packaged systems for pipeline, terminal, loading, metering and process applications.

Who we are

Engineered solutions, packaged product & process management

Our solutions are developed for demanding sectors including oil & gas, petrochemical, refineries, tank farms, aviation fuel, LPG, LNG, marine terminals and bulk liquid handling.

Metering & loading systems

The LNS-MS Series

Covers metering and loading skids for refined hydrocarbons, fuel oils, LPG and LNG. Depending on service, systems may integrate Coriolis metering, filtration, pressure and temperature measurement, batch control, ESD, grounding, overfill protection, vapour/BOG return and loading interfaces. Each skid is configured according to product characteristics, flow range, pressure, temperature, hazardous-area requirements and terminal operating philosophy.
