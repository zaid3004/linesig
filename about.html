About LineSig

Engineering behind every launch, run and receive.

We design, build and test pipeline pigging and process skid equipment so operators can run their lines with confidence.

Our story

Purpose-built for pipeline reliability

LineSig was founded to give pipeline operators a focused, dependable source for pigging and process skid equipment — not a sprawling catalog, but the exact systems that keep a line running: launchers and receivers, pig valves, pigs, metering skids, surge vessels and scrubber systems.

Every project starts with your pipeline's real operating parameters, not a stock template. Our engineering team reviews design intent before a single weld is made.

What guides us

Our values

  • Safety-first design review on every system
  • On-time delivery, with full factory acceptance testing
  • Clear documentation — drawings, datasheets, test certificates
  • Direct engineering contact, not a call center
