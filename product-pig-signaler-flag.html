02a — Product overview · Flag Type

Description

LINESIG Bi-Directional Pig Signalers are pipeline-mounted detection devices designed to provide positive indication when a pig passes through a pipeline in either direction.

The Flag Type Bi-Directional Pig Signaler provides a clear mechanical indication when a pig passes the installation point.

The pipeline-mounted trigger mechanism is activated by pig contact. The mechanical transmission then changes the position of the external flag, giving operators a positive visual indication that the pig has passed. The unit can be designed for manual reset after pig passage.

How it works

Operating principle

Normal position: before pig passage, the intrusive trigger projects into the pipeline bore while the flag remains in its normal indication position.

Pig contact: as the pig passes, it contacts the sensing element. Because the mechanism is designed for bi-directional operation, pig movement from either upstream or downstream direction can actuate the device.

Indication: Pig Passage → Flag Changes Position

Reset: following confirmation of pig passage, the mechanism can be reset according to the selected design.

Typical installation locations

  • Pig launcher — Installed downstream of the launcher to confirm that the pig has successfully entered the pipeline.
  • Pig receiver — Installed upstream of the receiver to confirm pig arrival.
  • Intermediate pipeline location — Used where operational procedures require confirmation that the pig has passed a specific pipeline section.
  • Bidirectional pipeline — Particularly suitable where pigging operations can occur in either direction.
