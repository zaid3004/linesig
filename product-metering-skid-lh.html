Product overview · LNS-MS-LH

Description

The LineSig LNS-MS-LH is a skid-mounted tanker loading metering system developed for refined and light liquid hydrocarbon products, designed for controlled and traceable transfer from storage tanks, transfer headers or loading pumps to road tankers, railcars or marine-loading systems.

The skid combines flow conditioning, Coriolis mass measurement, batch control, flow regulation and loading-safety interfaces in a compact package, prioritising clean single-phase flow, controlled batch loading, a safe vehicle interface and reliable transaction data.

Typical products include gasoline/PMS, diesel/AGO, kerosene, Jet A1, naphtha and similar refined hydrocarbons. For Jet A1 service, filtration, cleanliness, water management, sampling and material requirements are reviewed separately per the project specification, and PMS/gasoline applications can interface with a terminal vapour-return or vapour-recovery system.

Design

Key features

  • Coriolis mass flowmeter preferred
  • Product-specific strainer/filter
  • Air eliminator where entrained air may be present
  • Pressure gauge and pressure transmitter
  • Temperature transmitter where required
  • Pneumatic or electro-pneumatic flow/batch control valve
  • Explosion-proof batch controller
  • Static-grounding interface
  • Overfill protection interface
  • ESD pushbutton / shutdown interface
  • Loading-arm or hose connection, top or bottom loading
  • PMS vapour-recovery interface where required

Advantages

  • Direct mass measurement, no separate density compensation
  • Improved loading accuracy and repeatability
  • Reliable batch preset and termination
  • Compact loading-bay installation, reduced field piping/cabling
  • Suitable for multi-product terminals
  • Supports automatic tanker-loading operations
  • Easy integration with TAS and terminal dispatch systems
  • Dedicated clean-service configuration available for Jet A1
