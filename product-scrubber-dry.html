Scrubber system — dry scrubber

Dry scrubber

A chemical adsorption system utilizing dry media for neutralizing and removing gaseous pollutants — no liquid effluent to manage.

Overview

The dry scrubber captures acid gases and pollutants using a dry sorbent or adsorbent media bed instead of a liquid stream, avoiding wastewater generation and the corrosion concerns of wet systems. It's a low-maintenance option well suited to sites without effluent treatment capacity.

Key features

  • No liquid effluent to manage or treat
  • Lower maintenance than comparable wet systems
  • Replaceable dry media bed for simple servicing
  • Reduced corrosion exposure on downstream equipment
  • Compact footprint for space-constrained sites
