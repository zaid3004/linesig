Scrubber system — dry sorbent

Dry sorbent scrubber LNS-SC-DRY

Removes gaseous pollutants without a continuously circulating liquid system — low wastewater generation, suited to high-temperature gas treatment.

Product overview · LNS-SC-DRY

Description

The LineSig LNS-SC-DRY removes gaseous pollutants without a continuously circulating liquid system. A dry or semi-dry reagent is introduced into the contaminated gas stream, where pollutants react with the sorbent and form stable solid reaction products that are subsequently removed by filtration or dust-collection equipment.

Typical reagents include hydrated lime (Ca(OH)₂), sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃), lime-based sorbents and application-specific powdered reagents.

Best suited for

Sulfur dioxide (SO₂), hydrogen chloride (HCl), hydrogen fluoride (HF), acid gases, selected sulfur compounds, incineration gases, combustion exhaust, and processes where wastewater generation should be minimised.

Design

Key advantages

  • No continuous liquid circulation
  • Low wastewater generation
  • Compact process arrangement
  • Suitable for high-temperature gas treatment
  • Simple chemical-storage and injection concept
  • Compatible with bag filters and dust collectors

Technology: Dry reagent injection + gas-solid reaction.

Where it's used

Typical industries

Waste-to-energy, incineration, cement, lime, metals, chemical plants, furnaces, boilers and industrial combustion systems.
