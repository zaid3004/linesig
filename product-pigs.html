Pigs

Cleaning, gauging and inspection pigs, sized and configured to your pipeline's diameter, bore and service.

Overview

From routine cleaning runs to gauging plate verification ahead of an inspection tool, LineSig supplies pigs matched to your exact line specification — bends, tees, wall thickness and all.

Key features

  • Sized to your exact line diameter and wall thickness
  • Cleaning, gauging, foam and utility pig types
  • Configured for bend radius and reduced-port valves
  • Wear-resistant discs and cups for long run life
  • Bulk and one-off order quantities available
