02 — Product overview

Description

LineSig Scrubber Systems combine mechanical separation, gas-liquid absorption, chemical neutralisation, biological degradation and dry sorbent technologies to match different process conditions, contaminant types and environmental requirements.

The product family spans five distinct technologies — biological, wet chemical, dry sorbent, venturi and flue gas desulfurization — so each installation is matched to the actual contaminant, gas volume and discharge requirement rather than one generic scrubber design.
