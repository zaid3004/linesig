Scrubber system

Gas/liquid separation scrubbers built for reliable, low-maintenance operation ahead of compression, metering or flare systems.

Overview

LineSig scrubber systems remove entrained liquids and solids from gas streams before they reach compressors, meters or flare lines — protecting downstream equipment and improving measurement accuracy.

Key features

  • Vertical or horizontal vessel configurations
  • Mesh pad, vane or cyclonic internals per duty
  • Level instrumentation and automatic drain options
  • Sized to your gas flow rate and liquid loading
  • ASME-code construction with full documentation
