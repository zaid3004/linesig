Pig signaler

Confirms pig passage at launcher, receiver or in-line points — intrusive or non-intrusive, with manual or digital signal output.

IntrusiveNon-intrusiveManualDigital

Overview

LineSig Pig Signalers detect and confirm the passage of a pig through the line. Choose an intrusive design for direct in-line detection, or a non-intrusive design that avoids penetrating the pressure boundary. Each is available with manual (mechanical flag) or digital (electronic transmitter) signal output.

Key features

  • Intrusive or non-intrusive detection
  • Manual (mechanical) or digital (electronic) signal output
  • Suitable for launcher, receiver and in-line mounting
  • Digital units support remote/SCADA integration
  • Rated to match your pipeline's design pressure
