02 — Product overview

Description

LINESIG Pig Signalers provide reliable local or remote indication of a pig passing through a pipeline. They are installed on pig launchers, receivers and pipeline sections to confirm pig movement during cleaning, batching, inspection and product-transfer operations.

The units are available in intrusive and non-intrusive designs, with mechanical, electrical or combined indication options.

Operating principle

When a pig passes the installation point, it activates the sensing mechanism. The signaler then provides a visible indication and, where specified, an electrical signal to a PLC, SCADA, alarm panel or control system.
