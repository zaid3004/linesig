Surge vessel — bladder type

Bladder type surge vessel

Controls pressure surge using a flexible butyl rubber bladder inside the vessel, separating gas cushion from process fluid.

Overview

The bladder type surge vessel uses a replaceable butyl rubber bladder to keep the gas cushion fully isolated from the process fluid. This prevents gas absorption into the liquid over time and keeps pre-charge pressure stable, making it a reliable choice where fluid compatibility and long-term pressure retention matter.

Key features

  • Butyl rubber bladder isolates gas cushion from process fluid
  • No gas absorption into the liquid over time
  • Replaceable bladder for long service life
  • Stable pre-charge pressure retention
  • Suitable for crude, condensate and refined product service
