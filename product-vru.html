Vapour Recovery Unit (VRU) LNS-VRU

Vapour recoveryMulti-bay gantryESD integrated

Overview

LineSig Vapour Recovery Units (VRU) are designed to collect and recover hydrocarbon vapours generated during tanker loading and terminal operations, reducing atmospheric emissions while improving product recovery and loading safety. For multi-tanker gantry systems, vapour from individual loading bays is routed through a common vapour-return header to a centralized recovery package incorporating separation, vapour conditioning, recovery equipment, instrumentation and control, allowing the VRU to operate as an integrated part of the loading rack with batch, ESD and terminal automation interfaces for efficient, controlled and environmentally responsible terminal operation.

Key features

  • Collects vapours from individual loading bays via a common vapour-return header
  • Centralized recovery package with separation and vapour conditioning
  • Reduces atmospheric emissions and improves product recovery
  • Integrated with the loading rack: batch, ESD and terminal automation interfaces
  • Instrumentation and control for controlled, automated operation
