Surge vessel

Pressure surge protection that guards your pipeline, pumps and downstream equipment from water hammer and transient spikes.

Bladder typeCompressor typeDip-tube type

Overview

LineSig surge vessels absorb pressure transients before they reach critical equipment. Available in bladder, compressor-charged and dip-tube configurations, sized to your line's surge analysis.

Key features

  • Bladder, compressor-charged or dip-tube designs
  • Sized from your pipeline's surge/transient analysis
  • ASME-code pressure vessel construction
  • Horizontal or vertical orientation
  • Instrumentation package available for level and pressure monitoring
