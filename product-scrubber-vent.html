Scrubber system — vent scrubber

Vent scrubber

A compact unit used to capture and neutralize toxic or odorous gases released during venting or depressurization operations.

Overview

The vent scrubber is sized for intermittent, short-duration releases — protecting personnel and the surrounding area when a line or vessel is vented or depressurized. Its compact footprint makes it easy to locate close to the vent point it's protecting.

Key features

  • Sized for intermittent, short-duration venting events
  • Compact footprint for close-coupled installation
  • Neutralizes toxic and odorous compounds before release
  • Fast response for depressurization events
  • Minimal ongoing operating footprint between events
