Product overview · LNS-SC-VEN

Description

The LineSig LNS-SC-VEN is a high-energy wet scrubbing system designed primarily for fine particulate, mist and aerosol removal. Contaminated gas is accelerated through a Venturi throat where scrubbing liquid is introduced.

High relative velocity between gas and liquid creates very fine droplets that capture suspended particles and soluble contaminants, making it effective even on sub-micron droplets and difficult, sticky dust streams.

Best suited for

Fine particulate matter, dust, acid mist, oil mist, aerosols, smoke, soluble gases combined with particulate loading, and sticky or difficult dust streams.

Design

Key advantages

  • High particulate capture efficiency
  • Effective on sub-micron droplets and aerosols
  • Handles high dust loading
  • Simultaneous gas and particulate treatment
  • Resistant to sticky particulate service
  • Can be combined with packed scrubbers

Technology: High-energy gas-liquid impaction.

Where it's used

Typical industries

Metallurgy, foundries, mineral processing, fertilizer, chemical plants, dryers, furnaces, incineration, battery plants and process exhaust systems.
