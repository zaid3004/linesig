Product overview · LNS-MS-FO

Description

The LineSig LNS-MS-FO is specially engineered for tanker loading of medium- to high-viscosity liquid products such as fuel oils, base oils and finished lubricating oils. Unlike conventional light-hydrocarbon metering, these applications require careful consideration of viscosity, operating temperature, pressure drop and heating requirements.

Coriolis mass flow measurement remains the preferred LineSig approach wherever technically suitable; a positive-displacement meter may be evaluated as an alternative where Coriolis pressure drop becomes excessive because of viscosity, line size or very high capacity.

For high-viscosity products, the skid is sized based not only on normal operating conditions but on the highest credible viscosity at the lowest operating temperature. The design may therefore include steam, thermal-oil or electric tracing, insulation, heated filters, jacketed equipment, recirculation and warm-up lines. Typical products: fuel oil, heavy fuel oil, furnace oil, base oil SN150/SN500, bright stock, lube base stocks, finished lubricants and other high-viscosity petroleum products.

Design

Key features

  • Coriolis mass flowmeter preferred; PD alternative where justified
  • Low-pressure-drop basket/duplex strainer
  • Temperature and pressure measurement
  • Flow / batch control valve — sized at hot and cold viscosity cases
  • Explosion-proof batch controller
  • Heating/tracing and thermal insulation options
  • Recirculation / warm-up interface
  • Static grounding, overfill protection, ESD interface, TAS/PLC integration

Advantages

  • Stable loading across multiple oil grades when correctly temperature-conditioned
  • Reduced operator dependence through batch preset and controlled ramping
  • Viscosity-conscious hydraulic design lowers risk of slow loading and cavitation
  • Can be configured for base-oil blending, lube-oil dispatch or heated heavy-fuel transfer

Metering selection: PD metering is not the default LineSig position. Coriolis remains preferred where technically suitable; PD is retained as an engineering alternative for duties where line size, viscosity, pressure drop or project philosophy justify it.

Scope of supply

Standard scope of supply

  • Fabricated structural skid base frame with lifting provision
  • Inlet isolation valve — manual or actuated
  • Low-pressure-drop basket/duplex strainer, sized for cold-viscosity pressure drop
  • DP indication across filter where required
  • Pressure gauge and pressure transmitter
  • Temperature element/transmitter
  • Coriolis mass flowmeter — preferred standard
  • Flow control / batch valve, check valve where required
  • Explosion-proof batch controller
  • Local ESD pushbutton
  • Grounding and overfill interface
  • Heating/tracing package and recirculation/warm-up interface — optional

Standard process sequence

Inlet isolation → Strainer/filter → Air eliminator/gas separator where applicable → Pressure/temperature measurement → Coriolis mass flowmeter (or approved PD alternative) → FCV/batch control valve → Check/isolation → Loading arm or hose interface.
