Surge vessel — dip-tube type

Dip-tube type surge vessel

A dip-tube arrangement provides reliable surge control without a bladder or an active compressor system.

Overview

The dip-tube type surge vessel uses an internal dip-tube arrangement to manage the gas/liquid interface, offering dependable surge protection with fewer moving parts than a compressor-charged system and no bladder to maintain or replace. It's a strong fit where simplicity and low maintenance are the priority.

Key features

  • No bladder or active compressor required
  • Fewer moving parts for lower maintenance
  • Internal dip-tube manages the gas/liquid interface
  • Reliable performance across varying flow conditions
  • Available in a range of sizes and pressure classes
