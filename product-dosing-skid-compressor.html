Dosing skid — compressor type

Compressor type dosing skid

Uses a compressor-charged gas cushion to absorb pressure transients, available in horizontal and vertical configurations.

Overview

The compressor type dosing skid maintains its gas cushion using a dedicated compressor package rather than a physical barrier, allowing precise, adjustable pre-charge control. It is available in both horizontal and vertical configurations to suit site layout and footprint constraints.

Key features

  • Compressor-maintained gas cushion for precise pre-charge control
  • Available in horizontal and vertical configurations
  • No bladder to replace or degrade over time
  • Suited to larger surge volumes and higher duty cycles
  • Instrumentation package for pressure and level monitoring
