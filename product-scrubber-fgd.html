Scrubber system — flue gas desulfurization

Flue gas desulfurization (FGD)

An advanced gas cleaning unit designed for SO₂ and particulate removal from combustion exhaust streams in power and process industries.

Overview

LineSig's FGD system removes sulfur dioxide and particulates from combustion flue gas before it reaches atmosphere, mitigating acid rain formation and respiratory impacts. Wet, dry and semi-dry configurations are available depending on your exhaust volume, SO2 loading and site constraints.

Key features

  • Wet, dry or semi-dry configurations available
  • High SO2 removal efficiency for combustion exhaust
  • Particulate removal alongside acid gas treatment
  • Sized to your exhaust volume and sulfur loading
  • Supports compliance with emissions regulations
