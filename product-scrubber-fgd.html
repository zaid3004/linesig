Product overview · LNS-SC-FGD

Description

The LineSig LNS-SC-FGD is engineered for sulfur-oxide removal from industrial combustion and process flue gases. Depending on project requirements, the system can use wet limestone, lime, caustic, seawater, dry sorbent or semi-dry treatment technology.

Typical FGD technologies include wet limestone FGD, lime scrubbing, caustic scrubbing, seawater FGD, dry sorbent injection and semi-dry/spray dryer absorber configurations — with gypsum recovery possible for limestone systems.

Best suited for

Sulfur dioxide (SO₂), sulfur trioxide (SO₃), acid gases, combustion-generated sulfur emissions, boiler and furnace flue gas, and high-volume industrial exhaust.

Design

Key advantages

  • Suitable for very large gas volumes
  • High SO₂ removal capability
  • Multiple reagent technologies
  • Integrated oxidation and blowdown options
  • Gypsum recovery possible for limestone systems
  • Fully automated reagent and emission control

Technology: Sulfur absorption / neutralization.

Where it's used

Typical industries

Power generation, boilers, refineries, petrochemical heaters, cement plants, metal processing, waste incineration, marine applications and large industrial furnaces.
