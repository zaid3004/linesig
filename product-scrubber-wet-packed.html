Scrubber system — wet packed scrubber

Wet packed scrubber

A vertical packed column designed to remove acidic or basic contaminants from process gases using liquid scrubbing media.

Overview

The wet packed scrubber passes contaminated gas counter-current through a packed bed irrigated with a liquid scrubbing medium — typically water or a caustic/acidic solution — capturing particulates and gases like SO2 and HCl with high removal efficiency. It's the workhorse choice for demanding acid-gas and particulate duty.

Key features

  • High removal efficiency for particulates and acid gases
  • Packed bed maximizes gas/liquid contact area
  • Configurable scrubbing liquid for the target contaminant
  • Recirculation and blowdown system included
  • Corrosion-resistant materials for aggressive services
