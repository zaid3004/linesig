02b — Product overview · Dual Type

Description

LINESIG Bi-Directional Pig Signalers are pipeline-mounted detection devices designed to provide positive indication when a pig passes through a pipeline in either direction.

The LINESIG Dual Bi-Directional Pig Signaler combines a conventional mechanical flag with an electrical/digital switching output.

When the pig passes the sensing point, the mechanism simultaneously operates the local mechanical flag and activates the electrical signal contact for remote indication — giving both field confirmation and centralized monitoring through PLC, SCADA, DCS, ESD or terminal automation systems.

How it works

Operating principle

Normal position: before pig passage, the intrusive trigger projects into the pipeline bore while the flag remains in its normal indication position.

Pig contact: as the pig passes, it contacts the sensing element. Because the mechanism is designed for bi-directional operation, pig movement from either upstream or downstream direction can actuate the device.

Indication: Pig Passage → Flag Changes Position + Electrical Contact Changes State

Reset: following confirmation of pig passage, the mechanism can be reset according to the selected design.

Digital output

Electrical output — Dual Type

Depending on the selected configuration, the digital section may provide:

  • Dry contact
  • SPDT contact
  • DPDT contact
  • Proximity switch
  • Limit switch
  • NAMUR-type proximity output
  • 24 VDC switching interface
  • Relay interface

Final output type must be defined in the electrical datasheet rather than assumed as universal across all versions.

Control system integration

The Dual Type can interface with PLC, SCADA, DCS, ESD, Terminal Automation Systems, Pipeline Monitoring Systems and Pig Tracking Systems. Typical status tags include:

  • Pig Passed
  • Pig Arrival
  • Pig Departure
  • Signaler Activated
  • Signaler Reset
  • Device Fault, where supported by the selected electrical arrangement
