01 — Pipeline pigging systems

Pig launcher & receiver PLR-QC Series

Engineered pressure-vessel assemblies for safe insertion, launching, receiving and removal of pipeline pigs during cleaning, batching, inspection, dewatering and product-separation operations.

Description Features & components Specifications Applications Compliance Documents Get a quote

01 — Product overview

Description

LINESIG Pig Launchers and Receivers are engineered pressure-vessel assemblies used for safe insertion, launching, receiving and removal of pipeline pigs during cleaning, batching, inspection, dewatering and product-separation operations.

Each unit can be custom-designed to suit pipeline size, operating pressure, process fluid, pig type, installation arrangement and applicable project standards.

02 — Design

Key features

  • Horizontal or vertical configuration
  • Quick-opening closure for safe access
  • Reducer or barred-tee transition design
  • Pig signaller connection provision
  • Vent, drain, pressure gauge and isolation connections
  • Closure safety interlock options
  • Pig handling and lifting provisions
  • Skid-mounted or support-mounted construction
  • Corrosion-resistant internal and external coatings
  • Suitable for manual or automated pigging systems

Main components

  • Main barrel
  • Pipeline connection nozzle
  • Reducer or transition section
  • Quick-opening closure
  • Pig signaller
  • Kicker or bypass connection
  • Vent and drain nozzles
  • Pressure monitoring connection
  • Support saddle or structural skid
  • Safety interlock and closure accessories
