Pig launcher & receiver

The entry and exit point for every pig run — built around your pipeline's diameter, pressure class and product service.

Overview

LineSig pig launcher and receiver systems form the launch and reception point for every pigging operation. Each unit is engineered around your pipeline's diameter, design pressure and product service, with a quick-opening closure for fast, safe access. Pair it with a LineSig Pig Signaler to confirm pig arrival and departure.

Key features

  • Quick-opening closure for fast, safe access
  • Kicker and barrel sized to your pig train
  • Vent, drain and bypass connections as required
  • Davit or lifting arm options for larger closures
  • Full hydrostatic testing before dispatch
