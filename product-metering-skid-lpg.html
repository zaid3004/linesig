Product overview · LNS-MS-LPG

Description

The LineSig LNS-MS-LPG is a pressurised-liquid metering skid designed for safe and accurate loading of liquefied petroleum gas. The system integrates Coriolis mass metering, pressure and temperature monitoring, explosion-proof batch control, ESD functions, tanker grounding, overfill protection and vapour-return/equalisation interfaces.

LPG metering requires a dedicated hydraulic design because the product must remain in the liquid phase throughout the metering system. The skid hydraulic design maintains adequate pressure through inlet piping, strainer, meter, control valve, loading arm and tanker connection to reduce the risk of flashing and two-phase flow through the Coriolis meter. Typical products: propane, butane, propane/butane mixtures and other pressurised liquefied hydrocarbon gases.

Design

Key features

  • Coriolis mass flowmeter for pressurised liquid LPG
  • Pressure-rated skid piping, LPG-compatible materials
  • Pressure gauge and pressure transmitter
  • Temperature transmitter

  • Explosion-proof batch controller
  • Vapour-return / equalisation connection

Advantages

  • Direct LPG mass measurement, high loading accuracy
  • Reduced dependence on volumetric correction
  • Integrated vapour equalisation, ESD-ready architecture
  • Compact skid design suitable for custody-transfer applications where configured accordingly

Safety philosophy

Typical loading permissives

  • Tanker grounding healthy
  • Overfill system healthy
  • Correct product route established
  • Meter and batch controller healthy
  • ESD chain healthy
  • Loading arm connected
  • Vapour return ready
  • Pressure within acceptable range

Loss of a critical permissive can initiate controlled or emergency loading shutdown.
