Product overview · LNS-SC-BIO

Description

The LineSig LNS-SC-BIO is a vertical packed-bed biological gas-treatment system engineered for continuous removal of water-soluble volatile organic compounds and odorous industrial emissions. Contaminated gas passes counter-current through a continuously wetted packed section where pollutants are transferred into the aqueous phase.

Specialised microorganisms subsequently metabolise the absorbed contaminants into environmentally benign end products such as carbon dioxide, water and mineral salts — a sustainable, low-chemical alternative to conventional wet chemical scrubbing.

Typical contaminants

Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S), ammonia (NH₃), mercaptans, dimethyl sulfide, trimethylamine and other amines, volatile fatty acids, methanol, ethanol, acetone, formaldehyde, water-soluble VOCs and complex organic odours.

Process

How it works

  • Contaminated gas enters the lower portion of the scrubber
  • Gas flows upward through high-surface-area biological packing while recirculating liquid flows downward
  • Soluble contaminants transfer from the gas phase into the liquid film
  • Acclimated microorganisms metabolise the absorbed contaminants
  • A high-efficiency mist eliminator removes entrained liquid droplets
  • Treated gas exits through the upper stack connection

Design

Key benefits

  • High-efficiency odour and soluble VOC control
  • Biological treatment with reduced chemical consumption
  • Suitable for continuous 24/7 industrial operation
  • Corrosion-resistant vertical packed-column construction
  • Compact footprint
  • Integrated water recirculation, nutrient and pH dosing capability
  • Automated level, pH and recirculation control
  • High-efficiency mist eliminator
  • Low secondary waste generation
  • PLC-based automatic operation

Main system components

  • Vertical scrubber column, biological packed media
  • Packing support grid, internal spray distribution system
  • Mist eliminator
  • Recirculation sump, scrubbing-water circulation pump
  • Fresh-water make-up, water level indicator/controller
  • Nutrient dosing system, optional pH correction system
  • Drain/blowdown connection, process gas blower where required
  • Electrical control panel, PLC/HMI automation and instrumentation

Where it's used

Industries & applications

  • Municipal & industrial wastewater treatment — headworks, clarifiers, sludge tanks, thickeners, dewatering buildings, sewer ventilation
  • Chemical & pharmaceutical — process vents, reactor exhaust, solvent handling, water-soluble organic vapours
  • Food processing & rendering — cookers, dryers, waste handling, fermentation, odorous process areas
  • Pulp & paper — reduced sulfur compounds, mercaptans and non-condensable gases
  • Biogas & organic waste facilities — H₂S and odorous reduced-sulfur treatment
  • Composting & waste processing — ammonia, sulfur compounds and mixed biological odours
