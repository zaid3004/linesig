Scrubber system — bio scrubber

Bio scrubber

An eco-friendly, biochemical air treatment unit that uses microbial activity to remove hydrogen sulfide, ammonia and VOCs from exhaust gases.

Overview

The LineSig bio scrubber treats odorous and corrosive gas streams biologically, using a microbial culture to break down hydrogen sulfide, ammonia and volatile organic compounds before they're released. It's a low-chemical, low-waste option suited to wastewater treatment, food processing and other odor-control applications.

Key features

  • Microbial treatment removes H2S, ammonia and VOCs
  • Low chemical consumption compared to wet chemical scrubbing
  • Reduced waste stream versus conventional scrubbing methods
  • Suited to continuous, low-to-moderate concentration duty
  • Compact packaged design for straightforward installation
